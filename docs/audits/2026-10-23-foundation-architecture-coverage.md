Date: 2026-10-23. Baseline: `9fafdac`.
Status: All baseline topics and ADRs reviewed; implementation and successor qualification remain open.

## Why This Reassessment Exists

The [earlier chat-informed audit](2026-10-23-chat-informed-engine-architecture-review.md) checked
current owner defects and interface debt, but did not re-decide all architectural choices in the
conversation. Its local findings remain valid; its suitability verdict was too narrow to answer
the user's broader request. It must not be cited as a complete extension/foundation design review.

The user now explicitly requires every foundation topic in the supplied conversation to be
reconsidered, including layer changes, replacement interfaces and ADR amendments or supersession.
Existing Accepted ADRs describe the starting contract, not proof that it is the best future design.
Named product requirements are sufficient to motivate bounded design/tracer work; absence of a
finished consumer must not turn every extension question into indefinite deferral.

## Review Method And Completion Rule

Six read-only Codex lanes cover all 103 existing numbered ADR files exactly once as a primary
assignment. Cross-domain integration is the parent reviewer's responsibility. The assignment
inventory was checked for duplicates, missing IDs and nonexistent files: none were found.
Lane responsibilities do not reserve files or make their recommendations authoritative.

For each topic, the finished review must name the requirement, actual owner and constraints,
at least two meaningful alternatives, a preferred design and tradeoff, public caller shape,
ADR disposition, implementation/deletion impact and falsifiable acceptance. A current limitation
may be retained, but only with a design reason and an evolution seam, not merely Accepted status.

For each ADR, the final disposition is one of Keep, Amend, Narrow, Replace, Research, or Historical.
Research means an explicit unresolved choice with a bounded next experiment, not a generic
invocation of future evidence. Historical entries are not reactivated. Decision status and
implementation qualification remain separate.
