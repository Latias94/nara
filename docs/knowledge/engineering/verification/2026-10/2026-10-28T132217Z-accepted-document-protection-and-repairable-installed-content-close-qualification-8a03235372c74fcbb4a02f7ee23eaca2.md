# Result

Five existing Session acceptance seams capture the exact document's bound project-relative source
and owner content identity. An accepted change marks matching declared members immediately.
No-op, refusal, selection, preview and classification-only changes do not. Source owners retain
validation, inverse/history and revision publication. Existing persistence lanes retain a private
sticky touch across initial asynchronous reads and later management, without a second document
store. Undo to clean, restored baseline bytes, Save replacement and document Close do not clear
known member protection. Conservative ASCII-case matching supplies protection, not deletion proof.

Explicit management/adoption/observation confirmation accepts known facts before IO. Exact pending
bytes remain unchanged; failed or uncertain publication cannot undo confirmed protection.
Additional facts invalidate old content bases and form coalesced automatic successors. Automatic
work leaves the explicit attempt/outcome slot intact. Reads retain their start-basis protection;
actual weaker disk requires explicit original-basis repair. Bounded unpublished-registration
provenance permits repair after a failed first publication without accepting deletion of an
already admitted installation.

Save/Reopen guard before physical work. Unknown pending classification may first retain the
original owned checkpoint/request; persistence polling progresses classification and protection
before starting its IO. A later edit cannot refresh that checkpoint. Known managed pending or
failure returns Busy/RequiresReconcile. Initial unavailable records retain unclassified accepted
touches even after clean Undo, rather than losing them on Close. Whole Close settles protection
before irreversible Host/task shutdown, preserves a repairable Session on failure and continues
physical retirement after file Hosts have retired. Application content inspection and exact repair
reach the retained published Session while ordinary authoring is gated.

# Follow-up

Keep the full foundation Goal active. Source acceptance is memory protection; only accepted
publication supplies disk evidence. A crash before publication may lose facts, and Windows
parent-directory sync remains unproven. Fresh Open grants no historical creation/deletion
authority. Actual replacement-opening protection repair has source-routing review but no new
dedicated failure-injection execution here. Interactive locality/latency is unmeasured.

recovery and an honest all-stream content contract. Generic unlink, default-stream equality,
graph withdrawal and this protection record do not close that policy. Other native platforms,
