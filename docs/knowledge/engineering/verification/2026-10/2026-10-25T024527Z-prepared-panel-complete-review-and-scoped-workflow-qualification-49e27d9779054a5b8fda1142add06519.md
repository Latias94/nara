# Result

- Real independently compiled registered panels prepare and retain the actual unpublished edit
  or construction on non-active Scene/Prefab sources. Consuming ordered actions use the same
  owner candidate; construction callbacks return its actual successful local-to-stored mapping.
- Finite read-only preparation preserves complete query/source/revision/Schema and lifecycle
  admission. No public Session, mutable paint access, unsafe borrow split, candidate registry,
  extra history, tool-built inverse or universal asynchronous interface is introduced.
- Command batches are move-only. Built-in observations borrow the original-slot outcomes,
  including filtered holes, rather than cloning a temporary full report; an owned panel callback
  still owns its historical result. Result/receipt clones retain exact construction scope affinity.
- Refused candidates are retried without preparation and preserve exact publication/mapping.
  Candidate-bearing supersede, Close filtering, hide/retarget/reset/restore and project replacement
  preserve source revision/history, Inspector projection and disk; replacement incarnations do
  not receive predecessor callbacks. This is not a deep comparison of every in-memory source byte.
- Borrowed built-in observer regressions use actual frontend dispatch/settlement: gesture owner
  acquisition and retirement cancellation, structure completion at its original index and missing
  slot rejection, and latest Browser result across several retired-origin holes.
- The registered construction consumer now submits two retained candidates from one draft/basis:
  one real successful receipt, then an exact stale refusal without another receipt/revision/history
  entry or entity. After Reopen, public enumeration and root reads prove both actual preview IDs,
  values and mutual source-local references, separately from historical receipt resolution.
