keeps its old object and cannot silently refresh when the path changes.

- `Original` protects open working roots/dependants and expected-object transactions. Owned
  Scene/Prefab requests, schema-owner captures and escaped validations retain their actual proof.
- `ReadContent` validates fresh read-only dependencies by consumed bytes plus actual canonical
  key/kind/path/metadata readmission. It allows equivalent leaf replacement without retaining a
  handle for every file. Deferred targets authenticate no body. Reclamation can temporarily retain
  verified current exclusions without manufacturing historical replacement or deletion authority.

do not reopen or register the same proof twice. Unselected inputs drop before their storage and
handle capacity are refunded; performed work remains spent. A real 80-dependency workflow passes

increased, not the production handle limit. Final CP1 installation must explicitly transfer already
charged Original custody instead of charging it again as a new acquisition.
